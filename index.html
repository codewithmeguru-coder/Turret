Spotify to YouTube

Paste a playlist, check the matches, press play.

1Paste your Spotify playlist link

In Spotify, open the playlist → … → Share → Copy link to playlist. The playlist must be public (… → Make public).

Want to play your Spotify queue instead?
  1. In Spotify, open the queue and select the songs (click the first, Shift-click the last).
  2. Right-click → Add to playlist.
  3. Paste that playlist's link above.
